    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six phases, in the order that wastes the least time. Finish the story first, then the strangers, then sweep activities and collectibles, then the crime grind — and let the long counters tick up while you do everything else.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam. Read this before you start — it is the one thing that can cost you a second playthrough.
