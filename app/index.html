About this guide

What it is

Leonida Platinum is a free, open-source, offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files — no account, no network, no ads. Your progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file, imported back, or shared as a link.

How the guides are written

Every trophy's name, description, grade and PSN progress target comes from the official list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every entry. Each guide is written in our own words from that official description: what to do, where, when it can be missed, how hard it is and how long it takes. Where the official description does not pin down a place, the guide says so instead of inventing one — the game is a day old and some spots are not yet checked in-game.

Spoiler setting

Turn on “Hide story spoilers” and the names and descriptions of un-earned story trophies are hidden until you earn them. Earned trophies always show in full.

Offline

Every file this page loads lives under app/. It makes no network request at run time — turn the network off and it keeps working.
