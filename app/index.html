One playthrough is enough, says PowerPyx: about 200 hours to the platinum at difficulty 4/10. Follow the five stages in order. Story first, activities and strangers second, collectibles third, the 100% checklist last.

    Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be cleaned up after the story.

    Rules that keep a run safe

    • No cheats. PowerPyx reports that cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off until the platinum pops.
    • Meditate in Chapter 1. The first meditation spot, at Jason’s house, is offered in Chapter 1 and is gone after it. Do it the moment it is offered.
    • One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay function and no mission gold medals, so nothing story-related needs a second run.
    • Nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. The checklist appears under Progress, then Stats, after the story, and earlier progress counts.

    How this guide was built

    Every trophy name, description and grade is shown as PlayStation publishes it, taken from the official list as Exophase and PSNProfiles list it (the two agree on every name, description and grade). Every guide is written in this entry’s own words, built from the official descriptions, and names its sources beside it. Where a place or step is not pinned down yet, the guide says so instead of inventing one.

      About the tracker

      Progress is saved in this browser only (localStorage). Export writes a JSON file you can keep or move to another browser; import reads it back. The spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story trophies you have not earned yet. The guide works fully offline: it loads nothing over the network.
